# Tidal Vision

> Tidal Vision Products, Inc. is a Bellingham, Washington biotechnology and specialty-chemicals
> manufacturer, and the only commercial-scale US producer of chitosan — a non-toxic, non-hazmat,
> biodegradable industrial biopolymer upcycled from crab and shrimp shell waste. Its zero-waste
> Chitofining process extracts, modifies and formulates chitosan chemistries that displace PFAS,
> halogenated chemicals, toxic antimicrobials and persistent plastics.

Tidal Vision is a materials-science manufacturer, not a software or API company. As of 2026-08-02

## Business lines

- [Tidal Clear — water and wastewater treatment](https://tidalvision.com/water/): chitosan-based
  coagulants and flocculants for industrial and municipal water treatment.
- [Tidal Grow AgriScience — crop and soil inputs](https://www.tidalgrowag.com/): chitosan
  biostimulants and plant-health products for agriculture.
- [Tidal-Tec Biomaterial Science — performance materials](https://tidalvision.com/material-solutions/):
  chitosan biomolecular treatments for textiles and industrial materials, replacing PFAS/PFOA,
  halogenated chemicals and persistent plastics.
